I graduated valedictorian in 2025 — the culmination of every one of these years, not a separate achievement from them. It's easy to read a title like that as one moment; it was really <mark>a decade of showing up on the days I didn't feel like it.</mark> Consistency compounds the same way capital does — unglamorously, in the background, until one day the total is bigger than any single input could explain.
