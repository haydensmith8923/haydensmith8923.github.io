I went into a Pi competition feeling sharp and came out having met people operating on a completely different level. <mark>No matter how brilliant you feel, there's always someone living in a different intellectual dimension.</mark> That stopped being discouraging once I realized what to do with it: benchmark against the best in the room instead of the room you're used to. That habit is basically the job description in an industry stacked with people who all used to be the smartest person in their old room, too.
