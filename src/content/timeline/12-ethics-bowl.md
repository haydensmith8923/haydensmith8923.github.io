Ethics Bowl is a debate format with no clean winners. You're rewarded for the quality of your reasoning, not for tearing the other side apart. It trained a specific muscle: <mark>arguing a position rigorously while still taking the other side's case seriously.</mark> That turns out to be the difference between advising someone well and telling them what they want to hear, which matters most when the ethical read and the profitable read don't line up.
