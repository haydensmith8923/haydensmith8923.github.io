Ethics Bowl is a debate format with no clean winners — you're rewarded for the quality of your reasoning, not for tearing the other side apart. It trained a specific muscle: <mark>arguing a position rigorously while still taking the other side's case seriously.</mark> That's a rarer skill than it sounds, and it's exactly what separates advising a client well from just telling them what they want to hear — especially when the ethical read on a deal and the profitable read on a deal don't perfectly line up.
