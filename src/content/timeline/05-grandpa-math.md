I lost my grandpa and won a math competition in the same stretch of time. Grief doesn't pause for a trophy, and a trophy doesn't undo grief — I learned that <mark>a good day and a bad day can happen simultaneously</mark> and you still have to be present for both. That's not a comfortable lesson, but it's a necessary one for a job where a deal can close and a personal setback can land in the same week, and neither one gets to be the only thing that's true.
