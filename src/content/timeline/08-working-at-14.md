I started working in 2021. My family didn't have a lot of financial cushion, so contributing wasn't really optional. The biggest thing I took from it was realizing that <mark>hard work doesn't cost anything to start.</mark> I couldn't buy a head start, but nobody could stop me from outworking whatever was in front of me. I also didn't expect how good it would feel to see my own effort actually pay off.
