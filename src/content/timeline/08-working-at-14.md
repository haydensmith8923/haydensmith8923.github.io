I started working in 2021. My family didn't have a lot of financial cushion, so contributing wasn't really optional. What I took from it wasn't the money. It was realizing that <mark>hard work is the one input that doesn't cost anything to start.</mark> I couldn't buy my way into a head start, but nobody could stop me from outworking the problem in front of me, and that was fully mine to control. The part I didn't expect was how good it felt to watch my own effort turn into something I could actually point at.
