We moved to Tarpon Springs, a small Greek fishing town on Florida's coast with its own language, traditions, and unspoken rules that I hadn't grown up around. I learned <mark>what it was like to be different</mark> fast. You either learn the culture and adapt, or you stay the outsider. Walking into a room where I had to earn trust on someone else's terms stopped being intimidating after that.
