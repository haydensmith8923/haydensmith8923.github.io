We moved to Tarpon Springs, a small Greek fishing town on Florida's coast with its own language, traditions, and unspoken rules that I hadn't grown up around. I learned <mark>what it was like to be different</mark> in a room, fast. You either read the culture and adapt, or you stay the outsider indefinitely. Walking into a room where you have to earn trust on someone else's terms stopped being intimidating after that.
