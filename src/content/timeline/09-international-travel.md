My first international trip was less about the destination and more about the reminder that <mark>not everyone grew up with the same privileges</mark> — including some of the privileges I'd grown up thinking were universal. It's easy to build a worldview entirely out of your own zip code. Getting out of it, even briefly, is what makes you actually useful in a business that runs on capital, clients, and context that crosses borders every single day.
