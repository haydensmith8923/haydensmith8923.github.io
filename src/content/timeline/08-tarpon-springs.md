We moved to Tarpon Springs, a small Greek fishing village on Florida's coast — a tight-knit community with its own language, traditions, and unspoken rules that I hadn't grown up around. I learned <mark>what it was like to be different</mark> in a room, fast. You either read the culture and adapt, or you stay the outsider. That's a skill that transfers directly to walking into a client meeting, or a new team, or a country you've never worked in before, and needing to earn trust on someone else's terms.
