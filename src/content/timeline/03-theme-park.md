I got separated from my family at a theme park. I don't remember being scared so much as I remember being <mark>fine on my own.</mark> It's a strange thing to trace a career interest back to, but that instinct — stay calm, assess the situation, figure out the next right step without waiting for someone to hand it to you — is the same instinct that gets rewarded on a trading floor or in a deal room when the plan falls apart at 11pm.
