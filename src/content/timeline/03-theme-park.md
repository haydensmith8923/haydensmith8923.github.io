I was about four years old when I got separated from my family at Busch Gardens in Tampa. I don't remember being scared so much as I remember being <mark>fine on my own.</mark> Stay calm, read the situation, work out the next step instead of waiting for someone to hand it to me. That instinct is the one I still lean on when a process goes sideways at 11pm and the plan everyone agreed to that morning no longer applies.
