title: "Lost at Busch Gardens"
order: 3
---

I was about four when I got separated from my family at Busch Gardens in Tampa. I don't remember being scared so much as I remember <mark>thinking I'd be fine on my own.</mark> I obviously wasn't, but I stayed calm and tried to figure out what to do next instead of just waiting. That's still how I handle it when something goes wrong, whether it's a kitchen falling behind on a Friday night or a model that breaks the night before it's due.
