title: "Captain of a team I had just joined"
order: 11
---

I tried out for football in 2023 having never played a down, which made me the least experienced guy on the team by a wide margin. By senior year my teammates voted me <mark>captain</mark>, and that fall we made the playoffs for the first time in more than five years. Nobody hands respect to the new guy, so I earned it by showing up and outworking people at practice.
