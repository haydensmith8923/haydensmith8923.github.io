Getting invited into my school's gifted program was the first time I understood <mark>what it felt like to be challenged</mark> instead of just getting things right by default. Easy work never taught me much about what I could actually do. I've been chasing the harder version ever since, whether that meant a heavier class schedule or a sport I had never played.
