Getting invited into my school's gifted program was the first time I understood <mark>what it felt like to be challenged</mark> instead of just getting things right by default. Work that doesn't stretch you doesn't tell you anything about yourself. That's a lesson I've chased ever since — including into a field where the whole point is to seek out the rooms where you're not automatically the smartest person in it.
