title: "Walking away from baseball"
order: 10
---

I quit baseball after playing it for almost a decade. Nearly ten years of practices, travel teams, and identity wrapped up in one sport — and I still walked away from it. <mark>Sunk cost isn't a reason to keep playing a game that's stopped paying off.</mark> That's an easy thing to say and a genuinely hard thing to do when the "investment" is years of your own life, which is exactly why it's the same discipline that separates good capital allocation from bad.
