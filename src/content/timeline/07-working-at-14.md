<mark>I started working at 14.</mark> My family didn't have a lot of financial cushion, so contributing wasn't optional — it was just what needed to happen. It taught me the value of a dollar a lot earlier than most, and gave me an early, unglamorous education in showing up and delivering on someone else's timeline, not just my own — which is most of what a first-year analyst job actually is.
