Greek Sing runs on a fixed date. There's no pushing the show back if a backdrop isn't finished — the curtain opens whether the set is ready or not.

As Head Sets Coordinator, I ran a 15-person team responsible for building 9 unique backdrops in time to support a live performance with 60+ performers, in front of an audience of 500+. That meant sequencing the build so every piece was structurally sound and scene-change-ready before rehearsal, not after — and adjusting the plan in real time whenever a design changed, a material didn't work, or a deadline moved.

The job had almost nothing to do with painting sets and almost everything to do with making sure fifteen different people, working on nine different pieces, all arrived at the same finish line on the same night.
